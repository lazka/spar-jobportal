# SPAR Careers style guide

Use this guide for new careers pages that should feel like the [SPAR Austria careers homepage](https://jobs.spar.at/de_DE/SparCareers). It is a practical reference, not an official brand manual. The visual reference was checked in Chromium at 1440 px and 390 px; the typefaces and search states are based on the site's styles and markup rather than its loading placeholders.

## Color and type

| Role | Value | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| SPAR red | `#CD0404` | Primary actions, one line of headline emphasis, campaign backgrounds |
| Deep red | `#950007` | Hover treatment and deeper shades in campaign backgrounds |
| Pale red | `#FFF3F2` | Occasional tinted sections; keep most of the page white |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Main canvas, search bar, text on dark surfaces |
| Black / charcoal | `#000000` / `#333333` | Headlines / general text |
| Muted text | `#4D4D4D` | Supporting copy under cards |
| SPAR green | `#006131` | Footer with white text; not the main action color |
| Focus blue | `#1E3A8A` | Keyboard focus on light surfaces; use a contrasting ring on dark ones |

Use **TT Commons Bold** for headings (the site declares `TTCommons Bd`, with TT Commons Pro in its theme) and **Be Vietnam Pro** for body copy and controls. Use the matching bold italic for a single emphasized word such as *du*. Use approved font files or a sans-serif fallback such as Segoe UI or Arial.

| Element | Reference size | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Campaign H1 | 72 px | Bold, line-height around `1`; let it wrap or scale down if a narrow crop needs it |
| Section H2 | 56 px desktop / 44 px mobile | Bold, tight line-height; a second line in red works well for editorial sections |
| Card heading | 32 px | Bold, roughly 34 px line-height |
| Body copy | 16 px | Regular, roughly 24 px line-height for paragraphs |

## Page layout

- **Header:** Put the approved SPAR logo and career navigation in a slim bar. Over a campaign photo, use white navigation over a dark top fade. On mobile, collapse the navigation but keep **Alle Stellen** visible.
- **Hero:** Lead with one short, left-aligned white headline and real employee photography against strong red. On desktop, place the subject to the right and leave quiet space behind the text. Check the crop and text contrast separately on mobile. A single still is enough to get the look; a carousel is optional.
- **Job search:** Put a wide white pill near the bottom of the hero. On desktop, combine keyword, **Bundesland**, and **Berufsfeld** with subtle separators and a red **Jobs anzeigen** button. At phone widths, use a compact keyword search and keep the filters accessible on the results page. Give icon-only actions an accessible name.
- **Editorial sections:** Start on white with generous breathing room. For culture or benefits, use a centered H2 followed by three side-by-side photo cards on desktop; stack them on narrow screens. Use consistent image crops, about **12 px** corner radii, and a short title and paragraph beneath each image. Let the photography do the work rather than boxing every card in a heavy border or shadow.
- **Spacing:** Start with **40 px** side gutters at wide widths and **16 px** on phones. The homepage leaves **160 px** between the hero and first heading on desktop and **80 px** on mobile. Use less for a functional results page, where the jobs should be visible sooner.
- **Footer:** Use a full-width green block with white links. Group contact, company, and legal links so they remain easy to scan on mobile.

## Actions and copy

Use pill-shaped buttons and one clear primary action per section. Use red with white text on light surfaces, or a white pill on red. Keep secondary actions outlined or as text links. Add visible focus states and check contrast against the actual photo, not just the fallback color.

Write in Austrian German (`de-AT`) and address candidates directly with **du**, **dir**, and **dein**. Use short, concrete headings and labels like **Alle Stellen**, **Dein Einstieg**, and **Job Match starten**. Follow the reference's inclusive wording, such as **Schüler:innen** or **Mitarbeitende**. Choose authentic, approved photography and keep headlines as live text rather than baking them into images.

For a utility page such as the Inoffizielles SPAR-Jobportal, carry over the type, red primary action, white surfaces, and concise labels. Skip the oversized campaign hero if it would push the map or results out of view.
